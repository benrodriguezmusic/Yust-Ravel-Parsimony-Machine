Run the Yust_Ravel_2.0 to calculate and playback the pitches!
Change the rotation of the middle layer (the 18 quantizer) by customizing the increment value on line 26.
Not every unique increment has a unique pitch collection, but movement by those increments (360 degrees divided by 12, 7, and 3) will capture every pitch collection.

You should check out this article:
Jason Yust, "Tonal prisms: iterated quantization in chromatic tonality and Ravel's ‘Ondine’", Journal of Mathematics and Music, 2013
